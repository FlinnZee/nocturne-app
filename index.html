The film you started on the big screen, now in bed.

Turn on phone and tablet sharing and your PC becomes a small private cinema for the house. Scan a code once, and every phone on your Wi-Fi can browse your library and play it, picking up where the PC left off.

  • The PC prepares the video, so the phone just plays it.
  • Nothing leaves your home network.
  • Pausing on the phone saves your place on the PC.

Quiet by design.

Updates itself
New versions download in the background and install when you close the app. No setup screens, ever.
Your files stay yours
Your library and file paths never leave the PC. Only titles Nocturne recognises sync between your PCs.
No ads, no feeds
Nothing is pushed at you. Home is your own films, where you left them.
